Welcome to the Teleprompter

  1. Get your script in. Paste it straight into the big black area, or use Import Google Doc in the side panel (it stays synced as you edit the doc).
  2. Press Space (or Play) to start scrolling. A 3-2-1 countdown runs, then read the line at the blue guide line.
  3. Adjust on the fly. ↑/↓ change speed, ←/→ jump back or forward, and the mouse wheel scrubs anywhere.
  4. Make it yours. The ⚙ panel sets font, size, speed, colors, mirror, and fullscreen — your choices are remembered.

Tip: italics in your script show up as highlighted riff sections, and [bracketed text] becomes a dimmed stage direction that isn't meant to be read aloud. Press ? any time for the full list of shortcuts.
